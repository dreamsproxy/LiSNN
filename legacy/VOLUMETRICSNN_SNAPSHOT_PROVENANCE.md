## Scope

VolumetricSNN explores reconstruction of 3D volumes from depth maps, camera-pose and depth prediction, point-cloud processing, projection, and reservoir-displacement-inspired spatial representations.

Although it is not an executable language or cognitive substrate, it is retained as the spatial/volumetric experimental lineage previously associated with the broader LiSNN research roster.

## Isolation

No active LiSNN module imports from this snapshot. Its model, vision, plotting, and point-cloud dependencies remain donor-local and are not added to LiSNN core requirements.

## Update rule

Advancing this snapshot requires a deliberate donor revision, updated provenance, and validation that no active LiSNN code has developed a direct dependency on the donor tree.
